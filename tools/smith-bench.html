Physical lengths (mm)

Synthesizes the microstrip trace width (Hammerstad's equations) from Z0 (the reference field at the top of the sidebar), εr, and thickness, then converts every electrical length below into physical mm alongside it.

Circles are drawn at true size, including any portion outside the unit circle — that matters for reading unconditional stability.
